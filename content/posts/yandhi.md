Okay so I made a YANDHI. It should come as little surprise to anyone who has ever read this blog that I am a Kanye West fan[^1]; he is probably the most-mentioned reference in my repertoire, and hell if it’s not true that I make a lot of references—doubly so if you’ve met me in person, where I must have surely brought up Kanye. In fact the number of Kanye fans is higher than you might believe, largely because of the following seminal trademark quote:

> If you are a fan of Kanye West, you are a fan of yourself.

I’d like to think that it goes the other way around as well. Or perhaps you prefer the version by Chance the Rapper on *Ultralight Beam*, shortly before he, uh, released one of the worst hip-hop records of all time[^2]:

> I met Kanye West, I'm never going to fail

Anyway, everyone’s got their own point for when Kanye fell off[^3], but few would disagree that with the fact that the (mental) decline was certainly ongoing by the time 2018 and the Uganda sessions rolled around, even as the era was superficially his ‘happiest’ during that time. What his radical conversion to Christianity around this time was instigated by is hard to describe (my guess is a manic episode, as per usual) but it certainly resulted in him scrapping his already-announced album *Yandhi* (which had already been debuted on SNL)—the recording sessions of which had been ongoing for almost a year—and instead refurbishing some of the songs into the still-good-but-Kanye’s-worst-output-till-that-date *Jesus is King.*

The good thing is that a bunch of the *Yandhi* songs are still lying around, having been subjected to quite a number of leaks and live recordings[^4], and so Kanye fans can create their own ‘yedits’ that basically remix the album into a complete version that approximates how it ‘would’ have sounded had it been released—though of course it’s impossible to say what production swagger Kanye would have brought to it, since the sound of *Yandhi* is quite a departure from Kanye’s usual sound to a more spacey and futuristic style that combines razor-sharp electro R&B and gospel sounds with trap, though in a different form that he had previously explored? Though I suppose it still sticks to his regular paradigm of beats consisting of chopped-up vocal samples.

# anyway here’s yandhi

I’ve got 68mins and 14 tracks on mine, and I’ve been using appropriate yedits (along with a few production/tracklist changes of my own) instead of the raw recordings. 

{% detail(title="1. Chakras (ft. the Dream) [4:03]", default_open=false) %}

It’s unclear whether this was meant to be an opener or a closer (changes depending on the version) but to me this is always the opener that sets the vibe of the album.

{% end %}

{% detail(title="2. The Storm (ft. XXXTENTACION, Kid Cudi, Ty Dolla $ign & Ant Clemons) [3:56]", default_open=false) %}

The original version of *Everything We Need*, including X’s verse and cursing.

{% end %}

{% detail(title="3. New Body (ft. Nicki Minaj & Ty Dolla $ign) [4:48]", default_open=false) %}

Definitely one of the Yandhi classics, and was originally supposed to be on *Vultures*! 

{% end %}

{% detail(title="4. Alien (ft. Young Thug, Ant Clemons, Quavo & Kid Cudi) [7:53]", default_open=false) %}

Not a *Yandhi* original (it leaked in 2020) but fits well with the rest of the album. This is the full 2-part version; the first part was Young Thug’s *Godzilla*, but I reinstate it here.

{% end %}

{% detail(title="5. Spread Your Wings [3:06]", default_open=false) %}

Part of the July 2019 leaks.

{% end %}

{% detail(title="6. All Dreams Real (ft. Ant Clemons) [2:57]", default_open=false) %}

This is where I think my version of the album really moves from being a collection of songs to the more introspective medley of the second half, which is just banger after banger. Lovely cut.

{% end %}

{% detail(title="7. Hurricane (ft. Ant Clemons) [2:38]", default_open=false) %}

I much prefer this version over the one that came out with the Weeknd and Lil Baby; Ant’s voice is a much better fit, and the singular verse pieces together well at this point in the album. I also generally dislike the way The Weeknd sings ’80 Degrees,’ and I’m also sure Ant is saying ‘warm enough for me’ instead of ‘warm it up for me,’ which is a better lyric. Although I’d like a version with the choir in the chorus, though it’s heavier than the laid-back vibe of this *Yandhi*.

{% end %}

{% detail(title="8. City In The Sky (ft. Ty Dolla $ign, 070 Shake, Kid Cudi, The Dream & Desiigner) [6:02]", default_open=false) %}

Definitely the song most ruined by *Yandhi*’s scrapping and OMG the AI version that released on *Vultures II* is so bad it’s unforgivable; this particular version might be one of my favorite Kanye tracks, period, even for an unreleased, and I’ve found a particularly good yedit that has a beautiful trailing electronic outro.

{% end %}

{% detail(title="9. The Garden (ft. Ant Clemons & Big Sean) [3:54]", default_open=false) %}

Nice deeper cut that didn’t make its way to *Jesus is King* either, but fits well here.

{% end %}

{% detail(title="10. Amazing (ft. Ant Clemons) [3:33]", default_open=false) %}

Really beautiful Ant Clemons track (there’s no Kanye on this version, Ant is singing reference vocals for him, though it has the Sunday Service Collective), with Timbaland production. This is probably the song on the album most different from what it would eventually come to be, considering that Skrillex was supposed to be on it, too (also it would get a name change, considering Kanye has a song called *Amazing* on *808s & Heartbreak*). This one could release, though, still; Ty has shown some interest in re-recording it and they recorded some takes during their 2025 sessions, but considering the state of Kanye in 2025 I would be hard-pressed to believe that anything there is salvageable.

{% end %}

{% detail(title="11. Cash To Burn (ft. Ant Clemons & Kenny G.) [2:40]", default_open=false) %}

Probably my favorite song on the album. A true classic, if it were ever released; unbelievable choice of sample in Greenshade’s *Red Light* and beautiful vocals. Probably most representative of the sound of the album as a whole.

{% end %}

{% detail(title="12. We Got Love (ft. Teyana Taylor & Ms. Lauryn Hill) [3:47]", default_open=false) %}
Reinstated version with Kanye’s verse added in, one of the classic *Yandhi* tracks.
{% end %}

{% detail(title="13. Last Name (ft. Ant Clemons) [4:13]", default_open=false) %}
Another *Yandhi* classic, so lovely.
{% end %}

{% detail(title="14. Law of Attraction (ft. Dua Lipa, Clipse, Ant Clemons and Kenny G) [10:51]", default_open=false) %}
This is definitely not the version that would’ve made it to the album, but I wanted to preserve both the Dua Lipa intro and the Kenny G sax at the end, and turning it into a complete monstrosity really gives it close energy. It made it to *Use this Gospel*, which has to be one of the greatest downgrades in history.
{% end %}
